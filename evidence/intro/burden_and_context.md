# Evidence — INTRODUCTION (burden & context)

Source bank: docs/Phase2_Evidence_Bank.md. Verdicts inherited from Quad-Connector.

- **Adolescent childbearing burden (Ghana).** Adolescent (15–19) childbearing prevalence
  **12.3%** in Ghana (MICS 2017–18, n=2974); higher with lower education, Volta region, low SES.
  — Alhassan et al. 2024, *Biomed Res Int*, DOI 10.1155/2024/3237882 [SUPPORTING, body-confirmed].
- **Consequences of early childbirth.** Adolescent childbirth linked to later-life mobility
  disability across 14 LMICs. — Peck et al. 2023, *BMJ Open*, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2023-072535.
- **Fertility decline + determinants framing.** ~1/3 of transitioning SSA countries stalled;
  education & child-mortality decline drive sustained decline. — Shapiro & Tenikue 2013,
  *Afr Popul Stud* [SUPPORTING].
- **Subnational burden framing.** GBD 2023 subnational health-metric framework (660 subnational
  units). — GBD 2023, *Lancet* 2025, DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(25)01637-X [CONTEXTUAL].

- **Northern Ghana contraception baseline.** Upper East Region: contraceptive prevalence only
  **13%**, pervasive unmet need, demand dominated by spacing not limiting. — Bawah et al. 2019,
  *Reprod Health*, DOI 10.1186/s12978-019-0693-x.
- **Determinants framing (SSA).** Education + family-planning programs are the dominant drivers of
  fertility decline. — Bongaarts 2020, *Genus*.

Project ground-truth anchor (own data, verified): TFR fell from ~6.4 (1988) to ~3.5 (Ashanti)–
5.6 (Northern) by 2022; adolescent ASFR 15–19 fell from ~120–150 to ~40–90, persistently highest
in the northern savannah belt (outputs/data/_provenance_validation_fertility_16x9.csv).
