# Evidence — DISCUSSION (policy & debates)

## Debates to surface (never present as settled)
- **D1 — Is Ghana's fertility decline stalling?** Contested. Schoumaker 2019 (Ghana not a
  confirmed stall) + Machiyama 2010 (artefact). ✓ ≥2 CONTRASTING — present both sides.
- **D2 — Does an LSTM beat classical models for short demographic series? RESOLVED — likely NOT,
  for N=9.** Makridakis 2018 (ML dominated across M3) + Cerqueira 2019 (ML fails at low N) +
  Iaousse 2023 (ARIMA best small univariate) + Raftery 2012 / Yu 2023 (Bayesian is the calibrated
  subnational incumbent). ✓ ≥2 CONTRASTING. Frame the LSTM as one benchmarked option, never assert
  superiority; lead with interval coverage vs Bayesian/Lee-Carter baselines.

## Policy / translation
- Adolescent-pregnancy policy implementation in Ghana — barriers & facilitators (health/education
  professionals, grassroots, girls). — Ahinkorah et al. 2021, *BMJ Open*,
  DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2021-060576. Bridge forecasts → FP2030 / SDG 3.7 regional targeting.
- Intervention evidence: mHealth interventions improve adolescent SRH-service uptake in SSA
  (strongest for contraception); two-way interactive + behaviour-change-technique-rich designs work
  best. — Onukwugha et al. 2022, *PLoS ONE*, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0261973 (systematic review).
- Highest-fertility / highest-adolescent-fertility regions (project data: Northern, Savannah,
  North East, Volta-zone) are the policy-priority clusters → /policy-bridge once 2022 LISA confirms.

## Mandatory caveats (carry into Limitations)
- Inherited pre-2022 signal for 6 post-2018 regions (reconciliation §2) — forecasts parent-anchored.
- Short series (9 waves) stresses any sequence model; ensemble + interval coverage required.
- 2030 beyond next DHS (~2027) — widening uncertainty, report 2025 & 2030 with bands.
- Ecological/aggregate design — no within-region individual inference.
